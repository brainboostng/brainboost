Lesson Note

Biology is the scientific study of living organisms.

It covers their structures, functions, growth, reproduction, relationships and interactions with the environment.

Branches include Botany (plants), Zoology (animals), Microbiology (microorganisms), Ecology (organisms and their environment) and Genetics (heredity and variation).

Biology is important in medicine, agriculture, food production and environmental management.
