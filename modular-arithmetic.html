Modular Arithmetic

SS1 General Mathematics — First Term

What is Modular Arithmetic?

Modular arithmetic is a system of arithmetic that deals with the remainder after division.

It is sometimes called clock arithmetic because a clock repeats after reaching a certain number.

The Modulo Symbol

The symbol mod is used to find the remainder when one number is divided by another.

For example:

17 mod 5 = 2

This is because 17 divided by 5 gives a remainder of 2.

Another Example

23 mod 4 = 3

Since 23 = (4 × 5) + 3, the remainder is 3.

Quick Revision

  • Modular arithmetic works with remainders.
  • The word mod represents the remainder operation.
  • 17 mod 5 = 2.
  • 23 mod 4 = 3.
  • It is also known as clock arithmetic.
