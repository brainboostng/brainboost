Lesson Note

Living organisms show characteristics that distinguish them from non-living things.

Movement is the ability to change position or move body parts. Respiration releases energy from food. Nutrition is obtaining and using nutrients. Growth is a permanent increase in size or dry mass. Excretion removes metabolic waste. Reproduction produces new organisms. Sensitivity is the ability to detect and respond to changes.

A common memory aid is MRS GREN: Movement, Respiration, Sensitivity, Growth, Reproduction, Excretion and Nutrition.
