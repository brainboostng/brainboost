Lesson Note

Classification is the systematic grouping of organisms according to their similarities and differences.

It makes the huge variety of organisms easier to identify, name and study.

Biological classification uses groups such as kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus and species. Scientific names help scientists communicate consistently.
