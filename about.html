About BrainBoost

What is BrainBoost?

BrainBoost is a free learning platform created to help secondary school students learn, practise and improve their academic knowledge.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make learning simpler, more organized and more accessible to students.

Our Vision

We envision a learning platform where students can study useful materials, practise questions and develop confidence in their education.

Created as a Student Project

BrainBoost is being developed as a student web development project using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
