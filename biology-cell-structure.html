Lesson Note

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

The cell membrane controls movement of substances into and out of the cell. Cytoplasm is the site of many chemical reactions. The nucleus contains genetic material and controls many cell activities. Mitochondria are sites of aerobic respiration.

Plant cells also have a cellulose cell wall for support, chloroplasts for photosynthesis and a large permanent vacuole containing cell sap.
